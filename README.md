## 💫 About Me

Hi, I'm **Lemondeft** 👋

I'm a Computer Science vocational high school student from Indonesia focused on building practical software, exploring system architecture, and automating workflows.

Right now, I spend most of my time on **fullstack web development with TypeScript**, cross-platform mobile apps with **Flutter and Supabase**, and event-driven **automation tools with Baileys**. I'm also actively exploring AI integrations and machine learning.
